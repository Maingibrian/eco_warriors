🌱 Your five missions

How to Play

One small mission every day. By Friday you are a recycling hero!

  1. 1

    Build Your Bin!

    Grab a box. Make it yours. Stickers allowed!

    Ranger tip: cereal boxes make great bins!

  2. 2

    Pick Its Spot!

    Park it where the trash happens. The kitchen wins!

    Ranger tip: close to the action!

  3. 3

    Make a Big Label!

    Draw a huge sign: PLASTIC GOES HERE!

    Ranger tip: fat markers, HUGE letters!

  4. 4

    Grown-Up Check!

    Show a grown-up. Make sure they use it right!

    Ranger tip: rangers are polite but firm!

  5. 5

    Snap Your Proof!

    Take a photo of your bin — then log your win!

    Ranger tip: big smile, big bin!
