The 5-Day Challenge

Every day for five days you do one small mission at home. By Friday you have your very own recycling bin, a pile of plastic kept out of nature — and a badge to prove it. Ready? Steady? Let's go!

The five steps: build, place, label, check, scan.

Day 1

Build Your Bin!

Grab a box, decorate it, and boom — your very own recycling bin.

Ranger tip: cereal boxes make excellent bins.

Day 2

Pick the Best Spot!

Park it in the room where your family makes the most trash.

Ranger tip: the kitchen is usually mission control.

Day 3

Label It!

Draw a big, clear sign so everyone knows what goes inside.

Ranger tip: fat markers, huge letters, zero doubt.

Day 4

Parent Patrol!

Make sure the grown-ups use your bin the right way — no cheating!

Ranger tip: rangers are polite but firm.

Day 5

Scan & Show Off!

Scan the QR code to prove you finished — and claim your badge!

Ranger tip: find the Scan-me sticker on the poster.

Rainy-day mission

The Board Game, Too!

Eco Rangers is also a fast, physical board game about spotting waste types. A referee fires off questions while players race to grab the right card and jump to the matching bin on the board.

  • 2–5 players
  • 6+ years
  • Referee-led
  • Waste-sorting race
  1. 1The referee asks a question to all players.
  2. 2Players race to grab the card that answers it and guess the type of waste before time runs out.
  3. 3Correct? Jump or step to the square of the matching bin and place your card there.
  4. 4Hurry back for the next question — players who miss or are too slow wait for the next round.
  5. 5When the waste cards run out, the player with the most cards wins a challenge card.
